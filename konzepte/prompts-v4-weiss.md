Every panel is a photorealistic, high-end desktop screen state of the same award-winning website for a real German facility services company called BAU FACILITY SERVICES (Ratingen), spelled exactly B-A-U F-A-C-I-L-I-T-Y S-E-R-V-I-C-E-S. No clichés: NO hard hats indoors, NO thumbs up, NO handshake stock photos, NO cartoon icons, NO smartphones or tablets with screens. The nine panels read as ONE continuous story where each panel flows into the next.
